The people behind Axiom

Meet the Axiom team.

Axiom is being built by readers first: people who know the pleasure of stumbling onto a webnovel that stays with you long after its latest chapter.

Axiom began with a simple reader problem: free webnovels are everywhere, but the stories worth settling into are far harder to find.

Built by readers

wafflehunter and Shadowfax are Axiom’s co-founders. We started with a shared love of webnovels and a shared frustration with discovery that feels like an endless, noisy feed. We wanted a calmer way to find the stories that have real atmosphere, ideas, and characters worth following.

Readers, not gatekeepers

We care about the questions a story poses, the choices its characters make, and the emotional residue it leaves behind. That perspective shapes the catalogue, the profile system, and the conversations we want Axiom to host.

A place to linger

Axiom is meant to make thoughtful discovery feel personal. Find a novel, understand why it may suit you, follow the conversation around it, and return when you are ready for the next one.
