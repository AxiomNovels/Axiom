Meet the Axiom team.

A short note from wafflehunter.

By wafflehunter•October 6, 2026

Hi, I'm wafflehunter, a co-founder of Axiom! I started reading webnovels in 2019 and I got hooked almost immediately. I was mostly reading from Novel Updates, and I even remember spending entire weekends binging a novel series when I was really invested.

I would find a series I liked and just keep going until I ran out of chapters. A lot of the time I was looking through recommendations, checking tags, and trying to find something that had the same feeling as the last story I finished. Sometimes I would get lucky right away, and other times I would spend way too long looking.

That is basically where the idea for Axiom came from. I liked webnovels enough that I wanted to make a site that made finding them easier. I wanted it to feel less like scrolling through a huge list and more like finding something that actually fits what you are in the mood to read.

Right now I am also in university, finishing up my master’s degree in computer science. Axiom has been a fun way to combine the part of me that likes building things with the part of me that has spent years reading webnovels.

Off the page

When I am not studying or working on Axiom, I play chess casually from time to time. I am not super serious about it, but I like sitting down for a few games and thinking through the positions.

I am also pretty into health and skincare. I do some calisthenics, use sunscreen every day, and try to keep my routine consistent. I do not really eat sweets or junk food, I keep added sugar low, and I pay attention to sodium because it helps with facial bloating and keeping my skin clear. It is just something I have gotten used to doing.

That is a little bit about me and why I wanted to make Axiom. Hopefully it helps people find a webnovel they get really invested in too.
