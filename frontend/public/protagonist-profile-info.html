Axiom scoring guide

Understanding protagonist profiles

A protagonist profile measures how strongly six characteristics appear in a protagonist’s identity and decisions. Scores describe narrative evidence—not whether a trait is good or bad.

How to read the scale

0 is explicit absence

The story specifically establishes that the trait is absent, often as a meaningful part of the protagonist’s identity. Silence alone is not enough.

20 can mean limited evidence

The trait is outside the main storyline, barely discussed, or supported by too little evidence for a stronger conclusion.

50 is substantial

The trait appears repeatedly and clearly affects the protagonist, but does not consistently control their most important choices.

100 is defining

The trait is extreme, repeatedly demonstrated, and central to how the protagonist acts throughout the story.

Score and confidence are different. A low score means evidence points toward a weak or absent trait. Low confidence means there is not enough reliable evidence to be certain.
