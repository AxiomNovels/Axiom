How your result and matches work

Each answer contributes to an independent dimension, normalized from 0 to 100. Archetypes use different weighted combinations. Independence and unconventional thinking are measured directly for the Lone Wolf and Wildcard; they are decision patterns, not additional Axiom traits.

The highest eligible archetype wins. A second result within three points appears as an influence. Exact ties follow this fixed order: Mastermind, Natural Leader, Idealist, Guardian, Romantic, Conqueror, Lone Wolf, Wildcard. These weights and the three-point threshold are creative starting points, not scientifically validated cutoffs.

Protagonists are ranked by normalized Euclidean distance: D = √(Σ(your score − protagonist score)² / number of compared traits). Similarity = 100 − D. All candidates use the same dimensions; profiles missing any compared score are excluded. Similarity describes trait overlap, not a probability or a prediction that you will enjoy a novel.
