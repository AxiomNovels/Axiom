Axiom scoring guide

Understanding philosophy profiles

A philosophy profile measures how strongly major philosophical ideas and themes appear throughout a novel. Scores describe narrative evidence—not whether a theme is good or bad.

How to read the scale

0 is explicit absence

The story specifically establishes that the theme is absent, often as a meaningful part of the narrative. Silence alone is not enough.

20 can mean limited evidence

The theme is outside the main storyline, barely discussed, or supported by too little evidence for a stronger conclusion.

50 is substantial

The theme appears repeatedly and clearly affects the protagonist or story, but does not consistently control its most important ideas or decisions.

100 is defining

The theme is extreme, repeatedly explored, and central to the novel's ideas, conflicts, or protagonist throughout the story.

Score and confidence are different. A low score means evidence points toward a weak or absent theme. Low confidence means there is not enough reliable evidence to be certain.
