Analytics privacy

Optional reader analytics

If you allow analytics, Axiom records the pages you view, an approximate country, a random visit identifier, and estimated active time. When you are signed in, these events can be linked to your account. Authorized administrators can view account activity summaries.

Active time counts visible pages with recent interaction. It is an estimate, not a record of everything you read. We do not record keystrokes, private messages, passwords, or search query text.

Requests and network information

Axiom separately records server request counts, page or endpoint categories, response status, and approximate country to understand traffic and operate the service. These counts include automated traffic. Country is based on the network connection and may reflect a VPN.

When network logging is enabled, IP addresses are kept separately with a seven-day retention period and are accessible only through a restricted administrator view. IP addresses are not used to identify unique people.

Storage and your choices

Optional analytics use local browser storage for your preference and a visit identifier. A visit expires after 30 minutes without tracked activity. You can decline or change your preference using Analytics preferences in the footer. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals disable optional reader tracking.

Event and visit records have a 90-day retention period. Scheduled cleanup can add up to one hour before expired records are deleted. Declining stops future optional collection; it does not automatically delete earlier records.
