Axiom scoring guide

Understanding storytelling profiles

A storytelling profile measures how prominently seven narrative elements shape the reading experience. Scores describe emphasis, not quality.

How to read the scale

0 is explicitly absent

The story clearly avoids or excludes the element. A missing mention in a synopsis is not enough.

20 is light

The element appears rarely, remains peripheral, or has only limited evidence.

50 is substantial

The element recurs and meaningfully shapes the reading experience alongside other concerns.

100 is defining

The element dominates the novel's structure, conflicts, pacing, or appeal.

Score and confidence are different. Sparse evidence lowers confidence; it does not prove that an element is absent.
